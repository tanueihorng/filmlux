# Pack: handmade_story

**For:** human stories that should feel crafted, not corporate: culture, team spotlights, community, change stories, "a day in the life". 45–90 s.

**Defaults:**
- Theme `collage` (cut paper) or `riso` (two-ink print). Motion `stopmotion` (collage) or `mask` (riso).
- Transition `slide:from-bottom` or `wipe`.
- Music `calm` or `upbeat`. Voice Emma, Ava or a local voice (Luna, Wayne) at −4 %. Captions `narration`.

**Beats** (the story; choose a scene type and a `layout` for each: `python studio.py scenes`):
1. **Human hook**: who this is about
2. **Their world**: real photos
3. **Their words**: a quote
4. **What they do**
5. **Journey**
6. **Invitation**

**Treatments** (offer the user 2–3 before writing the script; each is a different structure, not only a different look):
- **Scrapbook**: prints, notes and tape: the story assembled on a table.
  `title` bottom → `polaroids` pile → `quote` note → `bento` pinboard → `timeline` steps → `end` left
- **Portrait**: one person, close up: big photos and their own words.
  `title` photo → `quote` side (with their photo) → `split` overlap → `note` sticky checklist → `end` center
- **Diary**: a handwritten thread from then to now.
  `note` card → `image` polaroid → `kinetic` stack → `timeline` vertical → `quote` huge → `end` band

**Signature scenes:** `polaroids`, `note`, `quote` layout `note`, `bento` layout `pinboard`, `split` / `image` layout `polaroid`. Use at least one; they make this pack look like itself.

**The original Shape** (still a valid treatment, the most conventional one):
1. `title`: a human hook ("Meet the team behind every <em>reply</em>")
2. `image` or `split`: real team photos (best) or AI collage illustrations
3. `quote`: a real person's words, with their role
4. `bento`: what they do, in their words
5. `timeline`: their journey
6. `end`: the invitation

**Craft:**
- Real photos beat illustrations here; cut-paper cards and grain make phone photos look intentional.
- Keep the stop-motion feel: no long camera moves, short 3–5 s scenes.
- For riso, two inks only: let the theme's pink and blue carry everything.

**Avoid:**
- stock-photo smiles; generated "real people" (never generate photos of people)
- corporate jargon in the narration
