# Pack: whiteboard_explainer

**For:** training, onboarding, policies and step-by-step how-tos that should feel friendly and hand-made, like someone explaining on a whiteboard. 60–120 s.

**Defaults:**
- Theme `whiteboard`. Motion `typewriter` (write-on). Transition `wipe`.
- Music `calm` (low) or `none`. Voice Ava or Emma −6 %. Captions `narration`.

**Beats** (the story; choose a scene type and a `layout` for each: `python studio.py scenes`):
1. **The question**
2. **Why it matters**: 3 points
3. **The steps**: 4–6, one verb each
4. **Do vs don't**
5. **A reminder worth keeping**
6. **Where to get help**

**Treatments** (offer the user 2–3 before writing the script; each is a different structure, not only a different look):
- **Whiteboard lesson**: drawn on as you watch.
  `title` left → `bullets` big → `process` → `compare` halves → `note` sticky → `end` center
- **Checklist**: a sticky-note checklist that ticks itself off.
  `note` sticky (checklist) → `annotate` side → `quote` note → `end` left
- **Ask the helpdesk**: the questions people really ask.
  `chat` center → `process` → `note` card → `end` band

**Signature scenes:** `note`, `annotate`, `chat`. Use at least one; they make this pack look like itself.

**The original Shape** (still a valid treatment, the most conventional one):
1. `title`: the question this answers ("How do I <em>escalate</em> a case?")
2. `bullets`: why it matters, 3 points
3. `process`: the steps (4–6), one verb each
4. `compare`: do vs don't
5. `quote` or `stat`: a reminder worth remembering
6. `end`: where to get help

**Craft:**
- Short, plain sentences; one step per screen when steps are tricky.
- Red and blue markers: put the key word in `<em>` and let the theme colour it.
- AI images (optional): ask for "whiteboard marker doodle" scenes; the theme's style already says this.

**Avoid:**
- dense text blocks; legal wording read out word for word
- more than six process steps (split into two scenes)
