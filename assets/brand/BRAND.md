# Filmlux brand

The Filmlux mark is a small projector throwing light (*lux*) into an **f**.

## Colours

| Colour | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Navy | `#00195F` | backgrounds, the wordmark on light |
| Cream | `#FBEDDB` | the wordmark on dark, light backgrounds |
| Coral | `#FB4E44` | the beam, accents |

## Files

| File | Use it for |

| `filmlux-campaign-logo-set/logo-for-light.png` | Full logo (navy wordmark) on light backgrounds |
| `filmlux-campaign-logo-set/logo-for-dark-recoloured.png` | Full logo (cream wordmark) on dark backgrounds |

| `filmlux-favicon.svg` | Browser tab icon: the beam on navy, readable at 16 px |

| `filmlux-brand-campaign-20261110.png` | Campaign poster |

## Rules

- Keep the beam coral on every background.
- Use the navy wordmark on light backgrounds and the cream wordmark on dark ones.
- Below 32 px, use the beam alone (`filmlux-favicon.svg`); the **f** becomes hard to read.
- Leave clear space around the logo of at least the height of the projector box.
